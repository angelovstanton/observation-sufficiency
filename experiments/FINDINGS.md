## CONFIRMED
_(full-corpus, passed control/statistics; each entry: claim — evidence — date)_

- **Removing volatile signals (B_noVolatile) improves grounding.** 22/24 pages B_noVolatile ≥ B_full; 18/24 strictly greater; corpus-wide +9.5pp (54.8% → 64.3%). The thesis ("volatile noise actively harms, not just costs tokens") holds across the full corpus, not just the page_21 dramatic case. [F0–F3 aggregate, 40 records/bundle/page, GPT-4.1 temp=0, 2026-06-19]

- **Semantic density is the dominant — and sole — primary moderator.** LOW-semantic pages: mean +15.0pp. HIGH-semantic pages: mean +4.0pp. Mechanism: with no stable semantic signal to prefer, the model defaults to the visually dominant token in the observation — frequently a volatile hashed class; stripping it removes the trap. **Survived a confound test against fingerprint type** (see REFUTED: fingerprint) — when fingerprint was the candidate alternative explanation, the effect collapsed into semantic density, not the reverse. This strengthens the finding: it withstood an attempt to explain it away with another variable. [2026-06-19]

- **Shadow depth does NOT moderate the effect.** Range only 2.5pp across S0/S1/S2 (S0 +8.4, S1 +10.9, S2 +9.1). Practical consequence: the cost-optimal profile (COP) recommendation needs no shadow-depth caveat — "strip volatile regardless of nesting." [2026-06-19]

- **Bundle frontier has a unique Pareto-optimal point: B_noVolatile.** Only rung that simultaneously (a) improves success above B_full (+9.48pp), (b) reduces tokens (7.5%), (c) shrinks the LOW/HIGH semantic gap from 15.00pp to 3.96pp. Token saving is small because volatile class strings are short tokens — the gain is from removing model confusion, not from compressing the observation. [Bundle ladder, F0–F3 aggregate, 5760 records, 2026-06-19]

- **B_noVolatile SHRINKS the semantic-density gap ~3.8× (15.00pp → 3.96pp).** Distinct from the moderator finding: this is a property of the INTERVENTION (it removes the handicap that made LOW-semantic pages hard), not just "LOW gains more." At every other informative rung the LOW/HIGH gap stays ≥12pp (B_full 15.00, B_noState 12.71, B_noSemantic 22.29, B_identityCore 22.92); only B_noVolatile brings it near 4pp. (B_minimalCore's 0pp gap is degenerate — it scores 0% on both strata, so there is no gap to close.) A large, one-rung reduction — but a 3.96pp residual remains, so this is NOT equalization: behaviour is substantially more uniform across page types under the COP, not uniform. [2026-06-19; gap figures reclassified 2026-07-20]

- **B_noState is a flat step** (0.3pp success change, 0.1% token change vs B_full). State attributes (disabled/checked/selected) carry no locator signal and cost nothing. Negligible on both axes — a clean negative result that simplifies the COP. [2026-06-19]

- **The thesis (irrelevant information actively harms) appears at TWO ladder thresholds, not one.** (1) B_full→B_noVolatile: volatile attrs harm (+9.48pp gain from removal). (2) B_noSemantic vs B_identityCore: B_identityCore (223 tokens, 39.58%) beats B_noSemantic (11,013 tokens, 35.31%) — the structural/volatile attrs retained in B_noSemantic are noise the model builds failing locators from, so B_noSemantic is Pareto-DOMINATED by a bundle with 50× fewer tokens. Frame as ONE thesis manifesting twice, not two separate findings. [2026-06-19]

- **B_minimalCore is the zero-information floor** (empty observation, 0/960 success). Confirms task descriptions alone carry no locator-resolving information — the model hallucinates plausible selectors that all fail. Valid baseline. [2026-06-19]

## CAVEATS / METHODOLOGICAL NOTES
_(true but require careful framing in the paper — reviewer ammunition if ignored)_

- **The "cliff" position depends on ladder ordering.** The biggest single success drop is B_noState→B_noSemantic (−19.79pp), but the ladder is CUMULATIVE and removes semantic BEFORE identity. The cliff is a property of the removal ORDER, not of semantic attributes in isolation — had identity been removed first, the cliff would sit elsewhere. Need a stated rationale for the ordering (increasing aggressiveness / decreasing removal-safety) before claiming "semantic is the cliff" in the paper. A reviewer will ask why this order. [2026-06-19]

- **Do NOT cite the 58.3% corpus-aggregate failure rate without context.** It aggregates ALL bundles including the deliberately-crippled ones (B_identityCore 91.0% lacked, B_minimalCore 0%). The COP (B_noVolatile×F3) success is 67.9%. Cite the COP rate, not the corpus aggregate, or the method looks like it fails when weak bundles are dragging the mean. [2026-06-19]

- **`model_grabbed_brittle_signal` is a residual bucket — do not read every member as a volatile grab.** Under the priority ordering (lacked > unreachable > grabbed) a record lands in `grabbed` whenever a stable signal was present and the locator neither succeeded nor was output-format-unreachable — i.e. any predicate-1–4 failure, not only volatile/positional ones. The dominant case is a genuine volatile grab (predicate_non_volatile false: **601 of 912** grabbed records); the remaining **311** used a stable signal yet failed to resolve (wrong element, non-unique match, or invalid cross-shadow syntax such as an invented `[data-shadow="true"]` attribute or a descendant selector crossing a shadow boundary). The name describes the dominant mechanism, not every member. [2026-07-22]

- **Axis C coverage is constrained by serverless TPM quota.** All non-OpenAI/non-Anthropic models (Llama-4-Scout, DeepSeek-V3.2, Mistral-Large-3) deploy at a 20K TPM serverless tier; a single large-page observation (page_17=110K) exceeds the per-minute budget → they cannot process the heavy pages. DECISION: rather than a partial result on easy pages, Axis C runs FOUR models — GPT-4.1, gpt-4.1-nano, o4-mini (COP-only), claude-sonnet-4-6. Claude itself (80K TPM) TPM-skipped page_08/17 + partial page_18, so cross-model numbers use the COMMON-EVENT subset (n=870). CONTINGENCY: TPM quota-increase requested for Mistral-Large-3 (~150K TPM); if granted, add as open-weight 3rd-family point. PAPER FRAMING: do NOT claim "model-agnostic"; claim "robustness across four models spanning two families, scale, and reasoning; broader open-weight generalization is future work, operationally constrained by serverless quota." Claude is the key independent (non-OpenAI) replication. [2026-06-20]

- **o4-mini "best for production" is NOT supported by this data.** o4-mini shows good success-per-token on the COP cell, but ran COP-only, and the token-only framework deliberately abstracts latency/throughput/cost-at-scale — what a production recommendation needs. Claim only "reasoning reaches the COP ceiling at moderate token cost; production trade-offs are out of scope." [2026-06-20]

- **ARIA-crossover base is thin.** The page_17/page_18 F4>NV crossover rests on 2 pages and 2–3 target reversals each. Real and mechanism-consistent, but underpowered for a standalone claim. Frame as observation + hypothesis, never as established routing rule, in this study. [2026-06-19]
