<h2 class="section-title">Frequently asked questions</h2>
<div class="faq-list">Can I change my plan at any time? Is my data safe with Axiom? Do you offer a free trial? What integrations do you support? Is there a minimum contract length? Can I import data from my existing tools? What kind of support do you provide? Can I use Axiom on mobile? Do you have an API? What currencies…</div>
<details>Can I change my plan at any time?</details>
<summary>Can I change my plan at any time?</summary>
<p>Yes — you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.</p>
<details>Is my data safe with Axiom?</details>
<summary>Is my data safe with Axiom?</summary>
<p>Absolutely. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and use AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is never sold or shared.</p>
<details>Do you offer a free trial?</details>
<summary>Do you offer a free trial?</summary>
<p>Yes — all plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.</p>
<details>What integrations do you support?</details>
<summary>What integrations do you support?</summary>
<p>We support 500+ integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Stripe, Zapier, GitHub, Jira, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and many more.</p>
<details>Is there a minimum contract length?</details>
<summary>Is there a minimum contract length?</summary>
<p>No — we offer month-to-month billing. Annual plans are available with a 20% discount if you prefer to pay upfront.</p>
<details>Can I import data from my existing tools?</details>
<summary>Can I import data from my existing tools?</summary>
<p>Yes — we provide free data migration support for all new customers. Our team will help you import contacts, projects, and historical data.</p>
<details>What kind of support do you provide?</details>
<summary>What kind of support do you provide?</summary>
<p>Starter plans have community support. Professional and above get email support with a 4-hour response SLA. Enterprise customers get a dedicated account manager.</p>
<details>Can I use Axiom on mobile?</details>
<summary>Can I use Axiom on mobile?</summary>
<p>Yes — we have native iOS and Android apps with full feature parity, offline support, and push notifications for task updates.</p>
<details>Do you have an API?</details>
<summary>Do you have an API?</summary>
<p>Yes — our REST API and GraphQL endpoint are available on Professional plans and above. Full documentation is available at docs.axiom.io/api.</p>
<details>What currencies do you accept?</details>
<summary>What currencies do you accept?</summary>
<p>We accept GBP, USD, EUR, AUD, CAD, and JPY. Currency is set at account creation and can be changed by contacting our billing team.</p>
<details>Is there a limit on storage?</details>
<summary>Is there a limit on storage?</summary>
<p>Starter plans include 1 GB. Professional plans include 100 GB per seat. Enterprise plans have unlimited storage with custom quotas available.</p>
<details>How does user management work?</details>
<summary>How does user management work?</summary>
<p>Administrators can invite users, set roles (Admin, Manager, Member, Viewer), and manage permissions from the Team Settings panel.</p>
<details>Can I customise workflows?</details>
<summary>Can I customise workflows?</summary>
<p>Yes — the workflow builder supports drag-and-drop automation with triggers, conditions, and actions. Templates are available for common use cases.</p>
<details>What happens when my trial ends?</details>
<summary>What happens when my trial ends?</summary>
<p>At the end of your 14-day trial you can choose a paid plan or stay on the free Starter tier. Your data is preserved either way for 90 days.</p>
<details>Do you offer educational discounts?</details>
<summary>Do you offer educational discounts?</summary>
<p>Yes — verified educational institutions and non-profits receive a 50% discount. Contact our team with your organisation credentials to apply.</p>
<details>How often do you release updates?</details>
<summary>How often do you release updates?</summary>
<p>We ship weekly product updates and release notes are published at axiom.io/changelog. Major releases are announced 30 days in advance.</p>
<details>Is there a sandbox environment?</details>
<summary>Is there a sandbox environment?</summary>
<p>Professional and Enterprise plans include a sandbox workspace for testing integrations and automations without affecting live data.</p>
<details>Can I white-label Axiom for my clients?</details>
<summary>Can I white-label Axiom for my clients?</summary>
<p>Yes — our white-label partner programme allows agencies and resellers to offer Axiom under their own brand. Contact partnerships@axiom.io to apply.</p>
<details>What reporting features are available?</details>
<summary>What reporting features are available?</summary>
<p>All plans include standard reports. Professional plans unlock custom report builders, scheduled email exports, and data warehouse integrations.</p>
<details>Is single sign-on supported?</details>
<summary>Is single sign-on supported?</summary>
<p>SSO via SAML 2.0 is available on Enterprise plans. We support Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and any other SAML-compatible identity provider.</p>
<details>How do I contact billing support?</details>
<summary>How do I contact billing support?</summary>
<p>Email billing@axiom.io or use the in-app chat. We aim to respond within 2 business hours on weekdays and 24 hours on weekends.</p>
<details>Do you offer volume discounts?</details>
<summary>Do you offer volume discounts?</summary>
<p>Yes — volume pricing is available for teams of 50+ users. Contact our sales team for a custom quote tailored to your organisation size and needs.</p>
<details>What happens to my data if I cancel?</details>
<summary>What happens to my data if I cancel?</summary>
<p>Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation. You can export everything in JSON, CSV, or PDF format at any time during this window.</p>
<details>Can I invite external collaborators?</details>
<summary>Can I invite external collaborators?</summary>
<p>Yes — you can invite guest users to specific projects. Guests have limited permissions and do not count toward your seat limit on paid plans.</p>
<details>Is there a public status page?</details>
<summary>Is there a public status page?</summary>
<p>Yes — real-time status and incident history are available at status.axiom.io. You can subscribe to email or SMS alerts for incidents.</p>
<details>Do you support multi-language interfaces?</details>
<summary>Do you support multi-language interfaces?</summary>
<p>Yes — Axiom is available in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, and Mandarin. More languages are added quarterly.</p>
<details>What payment methods do you accept?</details>
<summary>What payment methods do you accept?</summary>
<p>We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer (for annual Enterprise plans). Payment details are stored securely via Stripe.</p>
<details>How do I get started after sign-up?</details>
<summary>How do I get started after sign-up?</summary>
<p>After sign-up, our onboarding wizard guides you through key setup steps. You'll also receive a welcome email with video tutorials and our getting-started guide.</p>
<details>Can I run Axiom on-premises?</details>
<summary>Can I run Axiom on-premises?</summary>
<p>On-premises deployment is available for Enterprise customers with specific data residency requirements. Contact us to discuss your infrastructure constraints.</p>
<details>What is your data retention policy?</details>
<summary>What is your data retention policy?</summary>
<p>Active account data is retained indefinitely. Deleted items are soft-deleted for 30 days before permanent removal. Audit logs are retained for 2 years.</p>
