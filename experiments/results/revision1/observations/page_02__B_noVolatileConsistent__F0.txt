<h1>Apply for Housing Benefit</h1>
<p class="body">Housing Benefit can help you pay your rent if you are on a low income or claim certain benefits. Use this service to check your eligibility and submit your application online. You will need your National Insurance number, rent details, and your most recent bank statements.</p>
<div class="inset">Processing times are currently 15 working days. Apply as soon as possible to avoid disruption to your payments.</div>
