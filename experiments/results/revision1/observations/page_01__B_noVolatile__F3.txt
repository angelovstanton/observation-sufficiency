h3 {About Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules}
p {Amoxicillin is a broad-spectrum penicillin-type antibiotic used to treat a wide range of bacterial infections. It works by interfering with the formation of the bacteria's cell wall, causing the bacteria to die. Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are suitable for adults and children weighing 40 kg or more.}
p {This medicine is available without a prescription and is manufactured by Sandoz, a leading global generics company. Each capsule contains 500mg of amoxicillin (as amoxicillin trihydrate).}
div class=warning-box role=note aria-label="Important warning" {⚠️ Important: Do not take if you are allergic to penicillin or any other beta-lactam antibiotics. Seek medical advice before use if you have kidney disease or mononucleosis.}
