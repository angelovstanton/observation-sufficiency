<h3>About Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules</h3>
<p>Amoxicillin is a broad-spectrum penicillin-type antibiotic used to treat a wide range of bacterial infections. It works by interfering with the formation of the bacteria's cell wall, causing the bacteria to die. Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are suitable for adults and children weighing 40 kg or more.</p>
<p>This medicine is available without a prescription and is manufactured by Sandoz, a leading global generics company. Each capsule contains 500mg of amoxicillin (as amoxicillin trihydrate).</p>
<div class="warning-box" role="note" aria-label="Important warning">⚠️ Important: Do not take if you are allergic to penicillin or any other beta-lactam antibiotics. Seek medical advice before use if you have kidney disease or mononucleosis.</div>

<p>Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are indicated for the treatment of the following infections caused by susceptible organisms:</p>

<li>Skin and soft tissue infections</li>
<li>Dental infections and abscesses (in combination with metronidazole)</li>
<li>Helicobacter pylori eradication (in triple therapy)</li>

<p>Do not use Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules if you have a known allergy to amoxicillin, any other penicillin antibiotic, or any of the other ingredients of this medicine. <h3>Storage</h3>
<p>Store below 25°C in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Keep out of the reach and sight of children. Do not use after the expiry date printed on the carton.</p>

<h3>Method of Administration</h3>
<p>Swallow capsules whole with water. May be taken with or without food. If you have difficulty swallowing capsules, the contents may be dispersed in a small amount of water or mixed with food (e.g. yoghurt) immediately before taking.</p>
<h3>Overdose</h3>
<p>If you take more than prescribed, contact a doctor or Accident and Emergency immediately. In the event of overdose, adequate fluid intake and urinary output should be maintained to reduce the risk of amoxicillin crystalluria.</p>
