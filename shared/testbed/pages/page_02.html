Apply for Housing Benefit

Housing Benefit can help you pay your rent if you are on a low income or claim certain benefits. Use this service to check your eligibility and submit your application online. You will need your National Insurance number, rent details, and your most recent bank statements.

Processing times are currently 15 working days. Apply as soon as possible to avoid disruption to your payments.

Check an existing application

For example, HB-2024-001234

New application

It’s on your payslip, P60, or benefit letter. For example, QQ 12 34 56 C
Upload proof of identity, tenancy agreement, and 3 months of bank statements. Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG (max 10 MB each).

By submitting you confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete. Providing false information may result in prosecution and recovery of overpayments.
