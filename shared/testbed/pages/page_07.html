Frequently asked questions

Can I change my plan at any time?

Yes — you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

Is my data safe with Axiom?

Absolutely. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and use AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit. Your data is never sold or shared.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — all plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.

What integrations do you support?

We support 500+ integrations including Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Stripe, Zapier, GitHub, Jira, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and many more.

Is there a minimum contract length?

No — we offer month-to-month billing. Annual plans are available with a 20% discount if you prefer to pay upfront.

Can I import data from my existing tools?

Yes — we provide free data migration support for all new customers. Our team will help you import contacts, projects, and historical data.

What kind of support do you provide?

Starter plans have community support. Professional and above get email support with a 4-hour response SLA. Enterprise customers get a dedicated account manager.

Can I use Axiom on mobile?

Yes — we have native iOS and Android apps with full feature parity, offline support, and push notifications for task updates.

Do you have an API?

Yes — our REST API and GraphQL endpoint are available on Professional plans and above. Full documentation is available at docs.axiom.io/api.

What currencies do you accept?

We accept GBP, USD, EUR, AUD, CAD, and JPY. Currency is set at account creation and can be changed by contacting our billing team.

Is there a limit on storage?

Starter plans include 1 GB. Professional plans include 100 GB per seat. Enterprise plans have unlimited storage with custom quotas available.

How does user management work?

Administrators can invite users, set roles (Admin, Manager, Member, Viewer), and manage permissions from the Team Settings panel.

Can I customise workflows?

Yes — the workflow builder supports drag-and-drop automation with triggers, conditions, and actions. Templates are available for common use cases.

What happens when my trial ends?

At the end of your 14-day trial you can choose a paid plan or stay on the free Starter tier. Your data is preserved either way for 90 days.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Yes — verified educational institutions and non-profits receive a 50% discount. Contact our team with your organisation credentials to apply.

How often do you release updates?

We ship weekly product updates and release notes are published at axiom.io/changelog. Major releases are announced 30 days in advance.

Is there a sandbox environment?

Professional and Enterprise plans include a sandbox workspace for testing integrations and automations without affecting live data.

Can I white-label Axiom for my clients?

Yes — our white-label partner programme allows agencies and resellers to offer Axiom under their own brand. Contact partnerships@axiom.io to apply.

What reporting features are available?

All plans include standard reports. Professional plans unlock custom report builders, scheduled email exports, and data warehouse integrations.

Is single sign-on supported?

SSO via SAML 2.0 is available on Enterprise plans. We support Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace, and any other SAML-compatible identity provider.

How do I contact billing support?

Email billing@axiom.io or use the in-app chat. We aim to respond within 2 business hours on weekdays and 24 hours on weekends.

Do you offer volume discounts?

Yes — volume pricing is available for teams of 50+ users. Contact our sales team for a custom quote tailored to your organisation size and needs.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation. You can export everything in JSON, CSV, or PDF format at any time during this window.

Can I invite external collaborators?

Yes — you can invite guest users to specific projects. Guests have limited permissions and do not count toward your seat limit on paid plans.

Is there a public status page?

Yes — real-time status and incident history are available at status.axiom.io. You can subscribe to email or SMS alerts for incidents.

Do you support multi-language interfaces?

Yes — Axiom is available in English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, and Mandarin. More languages are added quarterly.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer (for annual Enterprise plans). Payment details are stored securely via Stripe.

How do I get started after sign-up?

After sign-up, our onboarding wizard guides you through key setup steps. You'll also receive a welcome email with video tutorials and our getting-started guide.

Can I run Axiom on-premises?

On-premises deployment is available for Enterprise customers with specific data residency requirements. Contact us to discuss your infrastructure constraints.

What is your data retention policy?

Active account data is retained indefinitely. Deleted items are soft-deleted for 30 days before permanent removal. Audit logs are retained for 2 years.
