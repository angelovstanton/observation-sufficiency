About Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules

Amoxicillin is a broad-spectrum penicillin-type antibiotic used to treat a wide range of bacterial infections. It works by interfering with the formation of the bacteria's cell wall, causing the bacteria to die. Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are suitable for adults and children weighing 40 kg or more.

This medicine is available without a prescription and is manufactured by Sandoz, a leading global generics company. Each capsule contains 500mg of amoxicillin (as amoxicillin trihydrate).

⚠️ Important: Do not take if you are allergic to penicillin or any other beta-lactam antibiotics. Seek medical advice before use if you have kidney disease or mononucleosis.

Active Ingredients

  • Amoxicillin trihydrate equivalent to 500mg amoxicillin per capsule
  • Magnesium stearate (E572)
  • Capsule shell: gelatin, titanium dioxide (E171), patent blue V (E131)

Indications

Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules are indicated for the treatment of the following infections caused by susceptible organisms:

  • Upper respiratory tract infections: ear, nose and throat infections
  • Lower respiratory tract infections: acute bronchitis, pneumonia
  • Urinary tract infections including cystitis and pyelonephritis
  • Skin and soft tissue infections
  • Dental infections and abscesses (in combination with metronidazole)
  • Helicobacter pylori eradication (in triple therapy)

Contraindications

Do not use Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules if you have a known allergy to amoxicillin, any other penicillin antibiotic, or any of the other ingredients of this medicine. Previous allergic reactions to penicillin may increase the risk of cross-sensitivity with other beta-lactam antibiotics such as cephalosporins.

Storage

Store below 25°C in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Keep out of the reach and sight of children. Do not use after the expiry date printed on the carton.

Recommended Dosage

Always take this medicine exactly as directed by your doctor or pharmacist. Do not exceed the recommended dose. The usual doses are shown below.

Indication Adults & children ≥40 kg Frequency Duration
Mild–moderate infections250mg–500mgThree times daily5–7 days
Severe infections500mg–1000mgThree times daily7–14 days
Urinary tract infection500mg–1000mgThree times daily7–14 days
Dental abscess250mg–500mgThree times daily3–7 days
H. pylori eradication1000mgTwice daily7 days
Community pneumonia500mgThree times daily5–7 days
Otitis media500mg–1000mgThree times daily5–7 days

⚠️ Doses should be spaced as evenly as possible throughout the day. Complete the full course even if you feel better. Stopping early can cause the infection to return.

Method of Administration

Swallow capsules whole with water. May be taken with or without food. If you have difficulty swallowing capsules, the contents may be dispersed in a small amount of water or mixed with food (e.g. yoghurt) immediately before taking.

Overdose

If you take more than prescribed, contact a doctor or Accident and Emergency immediately. In the event of overdose, adequate fluid intake and urinary output should be maintained to reduce the risk of amoxicillin crystalluria.

Can I take amoxicillin if I'm allergic to penicillin?

No. Amoxicillin is a type of penicillin antibiotic. If you have a known penicillin allergy you must not take this medicine. Speak to your doctor or pharmacist who can advise on suitable alternatives.

Answered by PharmaCare pharmacist · 10 Jan 2025

Can I drink alcohol while taking amoxicillin?

Alcohol does not directly interact with amoxicillin and will not reduce its effectiveness. However, alcohol can worsen some of the side effects such as nausea and dizziness, and it may slow your recovery. Moderate consumption is generally considered acceptable, but it's best to avoid alcohol while unwell.

Answered by PharmaCare pharmacist · 3 Dec 2024

What should I do if I miss a dose?

Take it as soon as you remember, unless it is nearly time for your next dose. In that case, skip the missed dose and continue as normal. Do not take two doses at the same time to make up for a missed one.

Answered by PharmaCare pharmacist · 18 Nov 2024

Is amoxicillin safe to take during pregnancy?

Amoxicillin is generally considered safe during pregnancy when used at recommended doses for appropriate infections. However, you should always consult your GP or midwife before taking any medicine during pregnancy.

Answered by PharmaCare pharmacist · 5 Nov 2024
